Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2025

Welcome to our Instagram Comment-to-Get Automation System (the "Service"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage when you interact with our Service through Instagram.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you participate in our campaigns by commenting on Instagram posts, we collect:

  • Instagram Username: Your public Instagram username
  • Instagram User ID: Your unique Instagram identifier
  • Comment Content: The text of your comments on our posts
  • Email Address: If you choose to provide it for content delivery (optional)

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We automatically collect:

  • Interaction Data: Timestamps, comment IDs, and post IDs associated with your interactions
  • Campaign Participation: Records of which campaigns you've participated in
  • Delivery Information: Delivery method, delivery status, and delivery timestamps
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser type, and device information when accessing our landing pages

1.3 Information from Third Parties

We receive information from Instagram's API, including your public profile information and comment activity on our posts.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Campaign Fulfillment: To deliver promised content (guides, coupons, links) when you comment with trigger keywords
  • Communication: To send you content via email, Instagram DM, or personalized landing pages
  • Service Improvement: To analyze campaign performance and improve our automation system
  • Compliance: To monitor and enforce compliance with our terms and Instagram's policies
  • Fraud Prevention: To detect and prevent abuse, spam, or fraudulent activity
  • Analytics: To track conversion rates, delivery success rates, and other performance metrics
  • Opt-out Management: To honor your requests to stop receiving communications

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process your personal data based on:

  • Consent: You consent to receive content by commenting with campaign keywords
  • Legitimate Interests: We have a legitimate interest in operating our service and improving campaign performance
  • Contract Performance: Processing is necessary to fulfill our commitment to deliver requested content
